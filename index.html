How to make Yummy Lime Shortbread Cookies:

By: Kailey

This recipe will tell you how to make quick and easy shortbread cookies with a lime icing on the top that taste delicious.

Ingredients

Cookies

  • 1 cup unsalted butter room temperature
  • ¾ cup confectioner's sugar
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 1 teaspoon lime zest
  • 2 ⅓ cups all-purpose flour
  • ¼ teaspoon salt

Icing

  • 2 ½ tablespoons lime juice
  • 1 cup confectioner's sugar
  • 1 teaspoon lime zest

Steps

Cookies

  1. In a bowl, blend together the butter and the sugar. Add vanilla and lime zest, and stir together,
  2. In a separate bowl, whisk together the flour and salt. Add dry ingredients to butter mixture and mix until a dough forms. Dough may look crumbly, but will hold together when pinched between two fingers.
  3. Shape into a long, round tube about 2 inches thick and wrap in wax paper. Put it in the freezer for about 10-15 minutes.
  4. Meanwhile, preheat oven to 350 degrees. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper and set it aside.
  5. Take out the dough,and cut it into many ¼ inch slices and place on the bakcing sheet.
  6. Bake for 10-12 minutes, then remove it from the oven and allow it to cool for 2-3 minutes before taking them off the sheet to cool completely.

Icing

  1. While cookies are cooling, combine confectioner's sugar, lime juice, and lime zest.
  2. Once your cookies have cooled completely, ice cookies with a butterknife, etc. and set on a cooling rack on a plate, and cool it for an hour.
